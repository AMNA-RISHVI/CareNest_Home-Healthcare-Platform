About Us

Our Mission

Making quality healthcare accessible to every family in Sri Lanka

Bridging the Gap Between Families and Healthcare

CareNest was founded in 2026 with a simple but powerful vision: no family should struggle to access quality healthcare. Whether it's an elderly parent who can't travel, a busy working mother who needs a pediatrician at home, or expatriate children worried about their parents' health - we connect them with verified professionals who come to their doorstep.

Headquartered in Colombo, we've grown to cover all 25 districts in Sri Lanka, serving over 50,000 families and partnering with 5,000+ verified healthcare professionals. Our platform handles everything from booking to billing, health records to real-time monitoring.

We believe healthcare should be personal, convenient, and stress-free. That's why we built a complete digital health ecosystem - not just a booking app, but a platform that manages your family's entire health journey.

Trust & Safety First

Every healthcare professional is rigorously verified with license checks, background verification, and credential validation before joining our platform.

Transparency

Clear pricing, verified reviews, and honest ratings. You always know who's getting and what you're paying for — no hidden fees.

Compassionate Care

Healthcare is personal. We prioritize professionals who show genuine empathy and treat every patient like family.

Innovation for Everyone

From Digital Health Passports to real-time overseas monitoring, we use technology to make healthcare smarter and more accessible.
